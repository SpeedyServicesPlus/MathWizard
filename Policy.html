Privacy Policy for Math Wizard

Last updated: November 19, 2025

1. Introduction

Math Wizard is designed for children and families. We are committed to creating a safe and trusted learning environment. Math Wizard does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app fully complies with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the Families Policy Requirements and Data Safety requirements.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Math Wizard does not collect or process any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, age, address, email, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device or identifiers (Android ID, advertising ID, IP address)
  • Usage data, analytics, or performance tracking
  • Photos, videos, or media on the device
  • Contacts, messages, files, or any other private information

3. No Third-Party Services

Math Wizard does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or social plugins.

4. No Internet or External Communication

The app does not require an internet connection, and it does not send or receive data to any servers beyond Google saving game progess if user grants google access. There are no external links, sign-in feature is for saving progress only, and no online connectivity beyond google saving your progress.

5. Children’s Privacy

Math Wizard is made specifically for children and therefore complies with children’s privacy laws and Google Play’s Families Policy. Because the app does not collect data, no personal information from children is ever requested, stored, or shared.

6. Permissions

Math Wizard does not request any permissions from the user’s device.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the “Last Updated” date above. All revisions will remain available for review.
