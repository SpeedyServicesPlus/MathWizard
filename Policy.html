Privacy Policy for Math Wizard

Last updated: November 19, 2025

1. Introduction

Math Wizard is designed for children and families. We are committed to maintaining a safe, private, and trusted learning environment. This privacy policy explains what information is stored and how it is handled. Math Wizard complies with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the Families Policy Requirements and Data Safety requirements.

2. Information We Collect

Math Wizard does not collect, store, or access any personal information from users. However, the app allows users to save their learning progress through their Google Account.

When a user signs in with a Google Account, Google may store basic app-related data such as:

  • Progress data (completed levels, scores, or learning milestones)

This data is stored securely by Google and is associated with the user's Google Account. Math Wizard’s developers do not receive, access, transfer, or use this information in any way.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Math Wizard does not collect or process:

  • Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (Android ID, advertising ID, IP address)
  • Analytics or usage tracking
  • Contacts, messages, photos, videos, or any files
  • Any personal data outside of progress stored by Google

4. No Third-Party Services

Math Wizard does not use any third-party SDKs, ads, analytics tools, tracking technologies, or external services. No data is shared with any external parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

Math Wizard is designed for children and follows all requirements of the Google Play Families Policy. The app does not request, collect, or use personal information. Any progress data is stored by Google only when a parent or guardian allows sign-in.

6. Permissions

Math Wizard does not request any device permissions.

7. Internet Use

An internet connection is only used if a parent/guardian chooses to sign in with a Google Account. No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Last Updated” date above.
